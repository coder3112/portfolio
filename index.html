01 eCVT: Electronic Continuously Variable Transmission

What?

  • Electronic CVT for the Baja SAE car that shifts 2x faster, led as Project Lead
  • Teensy 4.1 control, tested from gain-only through PID strategies.

How?

  • Built the dyno fixture and safety shielding, then wired the prototype on perfboard.
  • Integrating 4 sensors, reading at over 20K/s
  • 4-layer Altium PCBs

Results

  • 2x faster shifting, verified on the dyno.
  • Rotational inertia reduced 62% over mechanical CVT
  • EMI in the readings cut by over 80%.

Other work:

  • Debugged EMI and introduced shielded wire
  • Presented PDR and CDR
  • Wrote 5+ test plans

Control strategies tried:

  • Basic gain control
  • PID on the Teensy 4.1

03 Swarm Robots: Active Matter Research (CRAB Lab)

What?

  • 10 swarm robots built in one month for active matter trials: soldered, machined and programmed each.
  • RL for physical AI, optimal control of compliant multi-agent robots.

How?

  • Designed a 2 mm slip clutch to protect 20 DC servo motors.
  • Measured belt tension from calibrated motor PID torque instead of strain gauges.
  • Designed 6 gear belt spooling mechanism

Results

  • Stall incidents down 85%.
  • Belt spooling: 90% less slip, 35% less mass.
  • 50+ pose-tracked trials (40+ video hours), >95% accuracy
  • 250+ simulator runs.

Tools built:

  • Python/OpenCV pose tracking, calibrated to physical units
  • C++/MATLAB simulator with implicit integration

Test rigs:

  • Spring-loaded doors that open under adjustable finite push and close on release
  • Belt tension measurement from motor torque
